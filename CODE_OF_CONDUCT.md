<h1 align="center">Code of Conduct</h1>

<p align="center"><a href="README.md">← Back to the README</a></p>

Mentis is a project for students and for everyone who wants to learn. We want its community to be
welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever a person's age, experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

- Be kind and assume good intentions; many people here are beginners.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Respect different opinions and decisions, and the time of the volunteers.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised content, threats or publishing other people's
  private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, the translation
platform and any other channel of the project. The maintainers may edit or remove contributions
and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks it.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the contact on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/simozock). Every report is handled confidentially.
