# Sheets

A file can hold several sheets, each with its own map.

![The sheets at the bottom left of the window](../images/map/sheets.png)

## Add a sheet

Click **+** next to the sheet name at the bottom left (**Add sheet**), or press
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>. It starts with a mind map.

## Switch sheets

Open the list of sheets, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>[</kbd> /
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>]</kbd> for the previous / next one.

## Manage sheets

Right-click the sheet name, or **Main menu** › **Edit**:

- **Rename sheet…**
- **Duplicate sheet**: a copy with all its topics.
- **Delete sheet**: after a question; one sheet always stays.

To reorder, drag a sheet in the list.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting a sheet cannot be undone.

## Move a branch to a sheet

1. Right-click the topic where the branch starts.
2. Choose **Move to sheet** › **New sheet** or another sheet.

On a new sheet the branch becomes the central topic and names the sheet; on an existing one it
becomes a floating topic. Relationships to the rest of the map are removed.
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> brings it back.
