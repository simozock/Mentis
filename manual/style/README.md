# Style

Colours, shapes, fonts and lines, from one topic to the whole map, all in the side panel (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>]</kbd> opens its **Style** tab).

1. **[Topic style](topic-style.md)** — shape, fill, border, width, quick styles
2. **[Text](text.md)** — font, size, weight, colour, alignment
3. **[Branch lines](branch-lines.md)** — the lines from a topic to its subtopics
4. **[Color themes](color-themes.md)** — the colours of the whole map, and your own palettes
5. **[Map style](map-style.md)** — background, global font, lines and layout options
6. **[Copy and reset styles](copy-and-reset.md)** — format painter, copy, paste and reset
